합동 부분군 $\Gamma_0(N)$, $\Gamma_1(N)$, $\Gamma(N)$의 성질

Properties of congruence subgroups $\Gamma_0(N)$, $\Gamma_1(N)$, and $\Gamma(N)$


Notations

  • $\mathbb{N}$ is the set of positive integers.
  • $\mathbb{N}_0$ is the set of nonnegative integers.
  • $I_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ is the identity matrix.

Definition

  • For any positive integer $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $$\Gamma_0(N) := \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) \ \middle| \ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \equiv \begin{pmatrix} * & * \\ 0 & * \end{pmatrix} \pmod{N} \right\},$$ $$\Gamma_1(N) := \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) \ \middle| \ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \equiv \begin{pmatrix} 1 & * \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \pmod{N} \right\},$$ $$\Gamma(N) := \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) \ \middle| \ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \equiv \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \pmod{N} \right\}.$$ Here, $*$ denotes an arbitrary integer.
  • In particular, $\Gamma(N)$ is called the principal congruence subgroup of level $N$ for each $N \in \mathbb{N}$.


Basic properties

  • $$\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) = \Gamma_0(1) = \Gamma_1(1) = \Gamma(1)$$
  • For all $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $$\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) \geq \Gamma_0(N) \geq \Gamma_1(N) \geq \Gamma(N).$$ Moreover, we have \begin{align} [\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) : \Gamma_0(N)] & = N \prod_{p | N} \left( 1 + \frac{1}{p} \right), \\ [\Gamma_0(N) : \Gamma_1(N)] & = N \prod_{p | N} \left( 1 - \frac{1}{p} \right) = \phi(N), \\ [\Gamma_1(N) : \Gamma(N)] & = N. \end{align} Here, $p$ is a prime factor of $N$.
    $\phi$ denotes the Euler's totient function.

Degree, number of elliptic points, number of cusps, and genus

  • $N \in \mathbb{N}$ is a positive integer.
  • $\Gamma \leq \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ is a congruence subgroup.

  • $d(\Gamma) \in \mathbb{N}$ is the degree $\deg(f)$ of the natural projection map $$f: X(\Gamma) \to X(1), \quad f: \Gamma z \mapsto \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) z$$ of modular curves (as a holomorphic map between compact Riemann surfaces) . Thus we have $$d(\Gamma) = [\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) : \{ \pm I_2 \} \Gamma] = \left( \begin{cases} 1 & (-I_2 \in \Gamma) \\ \frac{1}{2} & (-I_2 \notin \Gamma) \end{cases} \right) [\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) : \Gamma].$$
  • $\epsilon_2(\Gamma) \in \mathbb{N}_0$ is the number of elliptic points of period 2 in $X(\Gamma)$.
  • $\epsilon_3(\Gamma) \in \mathbb{N}_0$ is the number of elliptic points of period 3 in $X(\Gamma)$.
  • $\epsilon_\infty(\Gamma) \in \mathbb{N}$ is the number of cusps of $X(\Gamma)$.
  • $g(\Gamma) \in \mathbb{N}_0$ is the genus of $X(\Gamma)$. We have $$g(\Gamma) = \frac{1}{12} d(\Gamma) - \frac{1}{4} \epsilon_2(\Gamma) - \frac{1}{3} \epsilon_3(\Gamma) - \frac{1}{2} \epsilon_\infty(\Gamma) + 1$$ (Theorem 3.1.1 of [Diamond-Shurman]) .

Notes
  • on $d(\Gamma_0(N))$, $d(\Gamma_1(N))$, and $d(\Gamma(N))$:
    • reference: page 106, 107 (and also page 101 for $d(\Gamma(N))$) of [Diamond-Shurman]

  • on $\epsilon_2(\Gamma_0(N))$:
    • reference: Corollary 3.7.2 of [Diamond-Shurman]
    • $$\chi_{4, 3}(n) = \left( \frac{-4}{n} \right) = \begin{cases} 1 & (n \equiv 1 \pmod{4}) \\ -1 & (n \equiv 3 \pmod{4}) \\ 0 & (\gcd(4, n) > 1) \end{cases} \quad (n \in \mathbb{Z})$$ is the unique primitive Dirichlet character of modulus 4. Here, $\left( \frac{-4}{n} \right)$ is the Kronecker symbol.

  • on $\epsilon_3(\Gamma_0(N))$:
    • reference: Corollary 3.7.2 of [Diamond-Shurman]
    • $$\chi_{3, 2}(n) = \left( \frac{-3}{n} \right) = \begin{cases} 1 & (n \equiv 1 \pmod{3}) \\ -1 & (n \equiv 2 \pmod{3}) \\ 0 & (\gcd(3, n) > 1) \end{cases} \quad (n \in \mathbb{Z})$$ is the unique primitive Dirichlet character of modulus 3. Here, $\left( \frac{-3}{n} \right)$ is the Kronecker symbol.

  • on $\epsilon_2(\Gamma_1(N))$, $\epsilon_2(\Gamma(N))$, $\epsilon_3(\Gamma_1(N))$, and $\epsilon_3(\Gamma(N))$:
