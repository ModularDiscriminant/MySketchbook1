Properties of congruence subgroups $\Gamma_0(N)$, $\Gamma_1(N)$, and $\Gamma(N)$



We denote the set of positive integers by $\mathbb{N}$.

Definition

  • For any positive integer $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $$\Gamma_0(N) := \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) \ \middle| \ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \equiv \begin{pmatrix} * & * \\ 0 & * \end{pmatrix} \pmod{N} \right\},$$ $$\Gamma_1(N) := \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) \ \middle| \ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \equiv \begin{pmatrix} 1 & * \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \pmod{N} \right\},$$ $$\Gamma(N) := \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) \ \middle| \ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \equiv \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \pmod{N} \right\}.$$
  • In particular, $\Gamma(N)$ is called the principal congruence subgroup of level $N$ for each $N \in \mathbb{N}$.


Basic properties

  • $$\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) = \Gamma_0(1) = \Gamma_1(1) = \Gamma(1)$$
  • For all $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $$\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z}) \geq \Gamma_0(N) \geq \Gamma_1(N) \geq \Gamma(N).$$
